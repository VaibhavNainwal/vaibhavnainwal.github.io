About

I am a Research Associate at the Centre for Advanced Financial Research and Learning (CAFRAL), Reserve Bank of India (RBI). At CAFRAL, my research focuses on the intersection of firms, workers, and economic policy, examining how firms and workers respond to policy interventions, with a particular emphasis on firm productivity and resource allocation.

Beyond this, my broader research interests lie in development economics, particularly issues related to income and wealth inequality and other socioeconomic challenges.

Working Papers

Inflation Targeting, Monetary Policy Surprises, and Capital Misallocation

with Vidhya Soundararajan and Rajeswari Sengupta

SSRN Working Paper ↗

Abstract

We examine whether a more predictable monetary policy framework improves the allocation of capital across firms. Using firm-level data from the Indian manufacturing sector for 2005–2024, we study this in the context of India's adoption of flexible inflation targeting (FIT) in 2015. We find that firms with high pre-FIT average revenue per unit of capital (ARPK) subsequently accumulate more capital and experience a relative decline in ARPK, consistent with capital being reallocated toward previously under-capitalized firms.
